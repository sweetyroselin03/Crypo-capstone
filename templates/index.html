Quantum-Ready Cryptographic Architecture

QR Code-Based Secure Key Exchange with Biometric Eye-Scan

A multi-layered Zero-Trust file sharing protocol combining PBKDF2 key wrapping, authenticated encryption (AES-128-CBC & Blowfish-CBC with HMAC-SHA256), active blink liveness verification, and periocular biometric authentication.

Sender: Encrypt & Share Receiver: Authenticate & Decrypt Benchmarks

Wrapped Key QR Exchange

Raw cryptographic keys are never embedded in plain QR codes. Keys + IVs are wrapped with AES-256-GCM under a password-derived key (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with 200,000 iterations).

Biometric Eye-Scan Layer

Webcam frames track facial and iris landmarks (MediaPipe Face Mesh). Normalizes eye crops, validates sharpness/lighting, and extracts 512D LBP + radial ring embeddings.

Anti-Spoofing Liveness

Real-time Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) challenge-response requires genuine eye blinks, thwarting high-resolution photograph and video replay attacks.

Strict 6-Step State Machine

Server-enforced session pipeline (QR_OK → PASSWORD_OK → KEY_SHOWN → EYE_ENROLLED → EYE_VERIFIED → DATA_VIEW). Unauthorized access attempts trigger HTTP 403.
